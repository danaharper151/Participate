# Privacy Policy for Participate

Last updated: June 6, 2026

Participate ("the app") does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

## Data Storage
- All data is stored locally on your device using Room database
- No information is sent to external servers
- Data is automatically deleted when you uninstall the app

## Third-Party Services
The app uses WebView to display content from SciStarter.org. Please refer to 
SciStarter's privacy policy for information about data collected when browsing 
their website.

## Changes
Any changes to this policy will be posted in app updates.
